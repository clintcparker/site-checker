**Status**: ⚠️ PARTIAL

1. Parse `ignore:` from `.github/dependabot.yml` and the `uses:` pin set from both workflows.
2. For each action, ask whether the next major *above* its pin is declined or reachable.
3. Separately, ask whether any declined version sits *at or below* its pin.

**Expected** (as asserted in run 4): every declined major sits strictly above its pin, so the block is

**Actual**: the second half passes 7/7 — `checkout` v8, `download-artifact` v9, `setup-node` v8,

moving the pins onto the declined versions. `main` now sits on the newest published major of every

opens by stating this ("every entry below is INERT, and not because of a syntax problem") and gives
the two reasons the entries are kept. The property US3 depends on — that no future major is
permanently silenced — passes.
