## Executive Summary

This is a fourteen-line mechanical change with an unusually well-documented audit behind it, and
the audit holds up. I re-derived every load-bearing claim in `tasks.md` from primary sources —
reading `action.yml` at each target tag and each *rejected* tag, plus the release notes for every
major crossed — rather than trusting the table. **Every target tag exists, every one resolves to
`runs: using: node24`, and every input the workflows pass is still accepted at the target ref.**
No pin was left behind: all 20 `uses:` lines across `ci.yml` and `release.yml` are accounted for,
`Swatinem/rust-cache@v2` (already node24, verified) and `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@stable` (composite)
were correctly left untouched, and `verify-install-channels.yml` genuinely contains no `uses:` at
all. `actionlint` is clean on all three workflows, all four files parse as YAML, and the four
constitution gates re-run green and unmoved (62 Rust / 47 frontend / clippy clean).

The two claims most likely to bite were the two I chased hardest, and both survive.
`actions/download-artifact@v7` still unzips by default and still defaults `merge-multiple: false`
— the decompression and hard-fail-on-digest-mismatch changes the audit table cites arrived in
**v8**, one major above the pin, so the `release` job's `files:` globs at
`artifacts/site-checker-<arch>/…` still resolve untouched. And `softprops/action-gh-release@v3`
still accepts all three inputs the release step passes, with `overwrite_files` still defaulting to
`true` — so the "re-run converges on one release object" property the file's own comment promises
is preserved.

**No blockers.** Two warnings and three suggestions, and the warnings are both about the *record*
rather than the pins. The new comment block at the top of `ci.yml` states a pinning rule — "the
lowest major that runs on node24" — that two of the six bumped actions deliberately do not follow
(W1); `setup-node@v5` and `pnpm/action-setup@v5` are *both* already node24, which I verified
directly, and `tasks.md` gives sound but different reasons for going to v6 on each. A maintainer
applying the comment's stated rule literally would downgrade both. And the new `dependabot.yml`
carries no memory of the "Deliberately Out of Scope" list it will start contradicting within a
week of merge (W2): its first PRs will propose `download-artifact@v8`, `upload-artifact@v7`, and
`checkout@v6` — the exact three majors this feature examined and declined.

**The condition is disclosure, not code.** Four of twenty tasks (T007, T015, T017, T020) are open
and none of them can be closed from a local checkout — they need a PR run, a real tag push, a
merge to `main`, and a post-merge run respectively. `tasks.md` says this plainly and correctly in
its "Why four tasks remain open" section. The condition is that the PR body says it too, so
"the pins merged" does not read as "the warning is confirmed gone." Nothing in this branch has
been observed on a live runner yet; every claim in it is verified by inspection.
