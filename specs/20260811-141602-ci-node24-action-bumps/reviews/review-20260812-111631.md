ignore, info, warn, error. Default is error which will fail the action." `warn` is a documented

confirm the rest of the comment block independently: "Hash mismatches will now error by default"
and "the action checks the `Content-Type` header ahead of unzipping and skips non-zipped files."
The `skips decompression for non-zip content` wording in `dependabot.yml` is accurate, not the
overstatement it could have been.

R003 is fixed in three of the four places it needed to be. `ci.yml`, `docs/how-to/release.md`, and
`CHANGELOG.md` now describe the pins `main` actually carries, and every load-bearing number in the
