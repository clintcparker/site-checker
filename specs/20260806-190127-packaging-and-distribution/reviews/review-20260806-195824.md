## Executive Summary

This is release engineering done carefully. The delivered half — one machine-written version
source, a headless-safe build, three workflow files, a cask template, and a how-to — is
internally consistent, matches its own contracts closely, and its load-bearing claims were
re-verified from primary sources during this review rather than taken on trust. Two claims in
particular that a reviewer would normally have to accept on faith turned out to be *correct* and
now have evidence behind them: `cargo test` genuinely does not need `../dist` (tauri-macros sets
`dev: cfg!(not(feature = "custom-protocol"))`, and with `devUrl` set the `frontendDist` existence
`panic!` in `tauri-codegen-2.6.3/src/context.rs:187` is never reached), and every Apple
environment-variable name in `release.yml` matches what the shipped Tauri CLI 2.11.4 actually
reads. All four standing gates re-run green and unmoved (55 / 30 / clippy clean /
`--frozen-lockfile` clean), and `actionlint` with shellcheck integration is clean on all three
workflows.

**No blockers.** The findings are six warnings and five suggestions, and they cluster in one
place: the *verification* half is weaker than the *publishing* half. `verify-install-channels.yml`
can't see a checksum drift between the tap and the release assets (W3) — which is precisely the
"green release, broken install" failure US4 exists to catch, and precisely the state a
half-completed re-run leaves behind per research R14. Its post-release tap check is also raced by
a CDN cache whose existence research R12 explicitly denied (W1, measured: `max-age=300`), and it
spends a `macos-latest` runner after *failed* releases because `workflow_run` has no conclusion
filter (W2). Two smaller hardening items sit in `release.yml`: the signing identity and the App
Store Connect key are materialised before `pnpm install` runs third-party lifecycle scripts (W4),
and `contents: write` is file-scoped where only one job needs it (W5).

**The conditions are about honesty in the ship record, not about code.** Fifteen of forty-seven
tasks are open, and by the spec's own FR-027 this feature is not "done" — no `v1.0.0` exists, the
repository is still private (verified: `isPrivate: true`), and no credential is set. That is not a
defect: T001 and T002 are a disclosure decision and a $99/yr purchase that are explicitly the
maintainer's, the plan sequenced the ungated work to land first, and `docs/ROADMAP.md` §2 already
records the reduced scope in exactly the spirit research R10 demands. The condition is that the PR
and ship record say so plainly rather than letting "the YAML merged" read as "packaging shipped."
